Account Deletion

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. How to request deletion

If you want to request deletion of your MonkisPlazii account and related information, follow these steps:

  1. Email peshistudios@gmail.com with the subject MonkisPlazii — Account deletion.
  2. Include your player identifier or sign-in method if available. Never send passwords.
  3. The team will verify that the request relates to your account and explain how it will be processed.

The request covers information associated with your game use, to the extent it is actually stored. Certain records may be retained for legal obligations or justified security purposes, with an explanation when applicable.

For more, see the Privacy Policy.
