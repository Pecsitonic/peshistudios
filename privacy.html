Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Peshi Studios, the independent development project behind MonkisPlazii, handles information when you use the Android game and its connected features. MonkisPlazii is an independent social game developed by Pecsitonic using Unity.

Privacy contact: contactopecsitonic@gmail.com.

2. Information that may be processed

The information involved depends on the features you use and the services enabled in your installed version.

  • Account and player identifiers: a player ID, authentication identifiers, and data needed to sign in or maintain a session.
  • Gameplay data: coins, crystals, character customization, items, rooms, progress, rewards, and minigame statistics.
  • Social features: room identifiers, chat messages, multiplayer interactions, and reports you choose to submit. Content shared in a room may be visible to other participants.
  • AI interactions: questions or messages you submit to Monki, the selected language, context needed to generate a reply, and AI-generated responses. Reports about AI-generated content may include the reported response and a player identifier when the reporting method provides one.
  • Technical information: game and operating system version, device model, usage events, crash reports, and technical identifiers used for diagnostics.
  • Purchases and ads, when enabled: transaction or reward events needed to validate purchases or ads; providers may also process device and advertising identifiers under their own policies.

3. How information is used

  • To authenticate players, store or restore progress, and operate game features.
  • To connect players, manage rooms, display messages, and investigate safety reports.
  • To generate responses during AI-powered interactions with Monki and review reports about inappropriate or offensive AI-generated responses.
  • To detect crashes, improve performance, and understand feature usage.
  • To process optional purchases and rewarded ads, where available.
  • To prevent abuse and protect the integrity of the service.

4. Third-party services

MonkisPlazii uses Firebase services for features such as authentication, analytics, and crash reporting, and may use Unity Gaming Services for multiplayer features such as lobbies and player connections.

If purchases or advertisements are enabled, Google Play services and the applicable advertising provider may also process the information required for those features. Providers maintain their own terms and privacy policies. The precise SDK inventory and Google Play Data safety disclosures are reviewed for each published version.

5. Chat, multiplayer, and reports

Do not post passwords, home addresses, financial details, or other sensitive information in chat. Your messages and display names may be visible to players in the same room. Reports may contain information used to review the reported behavior.

6. AI-powered interactions and response reports

MonkisPlazii offers AI-powered interactions with Monki. When you use this feature, the text you submit, your selected language, and the context required for the interaction are sent to the connected service that generates a response. The generated reply is returned to the game.

You can report an AI-generated response that you consider inappropriate or offensive through the in-game reporting feature. A report may contain the reported response, its language, and, depending on the reporting method, a player identifier. This information is used to investigate the report and address safety concerns.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or unexpected. Do not include passwords, payment information, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information in your messages or reports.

7. Retention, protection, and deletion

Information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain progress, investigate issues, or comply with applicable obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and service provider.

The service uses security capabilities provided by its vendors and reasonable access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

You may request information about or deletion of data linked to your account by emailing contactopecsitonic@gmail.com. See Account deletion for instructions. Specific data may be retained if required by law or for a justified safety purpose, with an explanation when applicable.

8. Children and younger players

Availability of the game and social features to younger players depends on the age rating, applicable rules, and the safeguards implemented. If you are a minor, ask a parent or guardian before sharing personal information or using online services.

9. International processing and updates

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards. We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revision date appears above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, personal data concerns, or deletion requests, email contactopecsitonic@gmail.com.
